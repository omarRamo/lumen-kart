Lumen, the little fox with the coral scarf, takes the wheel! Race through the glowing worlds of LUMEN in a 3D arcade kart racer built for thumbs.

EIGHT CIRCUITS, EIGHT WORLDS
Dawn Meadows, Coral Lagoons, Amazon Canopy, the Singing Dunes of Tunisia, Sidi Bou Said, Rooftop City, Aurora Night and the Realm of Night: every circuit has its own scenery, shortcuts and hazards.

DRIFTS, BOOSTS AND GLOWING ITEMS
Hold a drift through the corners: sparks turn blue, orange, then purple for a mini-turbo. Grab light prisms and throw seeds, fireflies, comets, night veils or shooting stars. Collect music notes to raise your top speed.

A CREW OF RACERS
Lumen, Zina the fennec, Pip the raccoon, Coralie the crab, Rivo the frog, Jagu the jaguar, Kibo the lion cub… and a mysterious rival to unlock.

GAME MODES
• Grand Prix: four races, points, podium and trophies for every cup and class
• Free race on any unlocked circuit
• Time trial and records
• 50cc to 200cc classes, plus Mirror mode

MADE FOR MOBILE
• Automatic acceleration, touch arrows or optional tilt steering
• Drift, item and brake under your right thumb, steering assist for beginners
• Optional vibration, automatic graphics quality, full-screen landscape
• English and French

PLAYER-FRIENDLY
• Fully playable offline, no account
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking
• No data collected: your progress stays on your device
