# Content rating — Lumen Kart

Target: **PEGI 3 / IARC 3+ · App Store 4+ · ESRB Everyone · USK 0**

## Content summary (for questionnaires and reviewers)
Cartoon arcade kart racing between anthropomorphic animals (a fox, a fennec, a raccoon, a crab, a frog, a jaguar, a lion cub)
and a starry "Night" rival. Mild, non-violent competition: karts bump, spin out on brambles, are slowed by seeds, fireflies
or a "sunflower burst" of petals, and recover immediately. No blood, no injury, no death, no weapons in the realistic sense,
no fear/horror (the Night world is indigo and starry, never dark or scary), no language, no sexual content, no drugs, no gambling
(item boxes are random in-race power-ups only, not purchasable, no loot boxes), no user-generated content, no chat, no ads,
no in-app purchases, no data collection.

## IARC questionnaire (Google Play) — expected answers
- Category: **Game**
- Violence: **No** to all realistic/blood/gore questions. Cartoon/fantasy: "mild, non-realistic action between cartoon characters
  where no one is hurt" if asked → still PEGI 3.
- Fear: No. Sexuality: No. Language: No. Controlled substances: No. Crude humour: No.
- Gambling / simulated gambling: **No** (random items are a racing mechanic, no stakes, no currency).
- Interactivity: users interact online? **No** (store builds have no online mode). Shares location? No. Digital purchases? **No**.
- Miscellaneous: unrestricted internet access? **No**.
